1. Executive Summary

Neo Gen Technologies proposes developing a custom AI-powered mortgage lead engagement and texting platform designed to automate communication, qualification, nurturing, and conversion of mortgage prospects for Green Lending.

Unlike conventional SMS marketing tools that send rigid, pre-written blast messages, this platform deploys a tailored conversational AI engine capable of understanding incoming borrower responses, maintaining context, asking mortgage-specific qualification questions, and instantly routing hot prospects to your licensed loan officers.

Target Mortgage Lead Workflows:
  • HELOC (Home Equity Lines of Credit): Property value, existing mortgage balance, accessible equity, borrowing need, purpose of funds.
  • Mortgage Refinancing: Current balance, current interest rate, target monthly savings, credit profile, refinancing timeline.
  • Home Purchase: Target budget, location, down payment readiness, pre-approval letter status, agent representation.
